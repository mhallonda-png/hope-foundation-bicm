Who We Are

Humanitarian service rooted in long-term commitment.

Hope Foundation-BICM has supported mission and humanitarian work in Kenya, including clean-water initiatives. Our current direction expands that foundation into integrated water, WASH, nutrition, education and community-resilience programming.

We combine field reporting, responsible stewardship, technical assessment and community participation to develop projects that can be measured, maintained and improved over time.

Our Kenya Heritage

Clean water has been central to the mission from the beginning.

The original Hope Foundation-BICM website documents a mission centered on drilling wells and improving access to clean drinking water in Kenya, including work focused on Samburu County. Project #1 • Turkana County

Kalotum School & Community Resilience Initiative

A pilot integrating water security, school feeding, WASH, education infrastructure and evidence-based humanitarian management.

The need

Current school records report 238 enrolled children. Village elders estimate approximately 680 children ages 5–16 in the wider community; that broader estimate is not school enrollment and will be verified during baseline work.

Water

The planned scope is a new borehole and solar pumping system with storage and distribution, guided by hydrogeological assessment, test pumping and water-quality analysis.

School infrastructure

The working program includes four classrooms, an eight-door ablution block, and a complete dining hall and kitchen, subject to contractor reconciliation and final procurement.

Nutrition

The Turkana Field Team developed a school-feeding plan for 238 children, with breakfast porridge and weekday lunch. The current annual planning allocation is $32,300 including contingency.

Current working subtotal$220,744

Major infrastructure + annual feeding allocation. Remaining non-duplicative monitoring, safeguarding, logistics and allowable administration are still being finalized.

Responsible Humanitarian Intelligence

Global Humanitarian & Water Intelligence Platform

GHWIP is Hope Foundation-BICM’s planned evidence and monitoring layer for understanding needs, tracking infrastructure, documenting field operations and measuring outcomes.

Communities→Needs→Projects→Monitoring→Outcomes

The platform is intended for humanitarian and development purposes—not military intelligence, political targeting or intrusive surveillance. High-impact decisions remain subject to human review.
